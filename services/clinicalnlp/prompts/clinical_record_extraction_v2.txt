Return one grounded emergency clinical record from the whole supplied dialogue.

Input segments contain id, start, end, raw_text, and corrected_text only when an approved deterministic correction differs from RAW. Segment text is immutable evidence. Read the whole supplied dialogue because segments normally have no speaker labels; link questions and answers by order and meaning. Preserve negation, uncertainty, questions, numbers, units, and medication wording. Never infer a finding merely because a clinician asked about it.

Extraction rules
- Never guess or add a fact absent from the dialogue. Omitted or unasked information must stay omitted for program-side NOT_ASSESSED normalization.
- Keep the source clinical wording verbatim in raw_value. Do not paraphrase, broaden, or silently standardize it.
- Use corrected_text only when present, but raw_value must remain a meaningful phrase supported by one segment.
- Split a mixed segment into the smallest complete supported facts before assigning fields. One segment may support several fields, but do not copy the same clause into multiple fields merely to fill them.
- chief_complaint is limited to the one or two explicit primary reasons for this emergency visit. The explicit symptom answer after an opening discomfort question belongs here; other current-illness details do not.
- history_of_present_illness contains onset, course, change, aggravating or relieving context, and associated symptoms of the current problem. Do not place past diagnoses, observed examination findings, assessments, or plans here.
- Keep onset to its time phrase. Preserve explicit negative and uncertain answers.
- Pain assessment uses explicit NRS only. Do not infer an NRS score from pain adjectives, location, or quality; omit non-NRS pain fields.
- Past history may include both medical and surgical history. Put diagnosed or denied diseases in underlying_conditions and actual operations/procedures in surgery_history.
- medications contains current medication use or explicitly reported administration. treatment_plan contains a stated future or current order for a test, medication, procedure, or device. Do not confuse either with a historical treatment.
- Do not emit a bare generic noun such as "약", "증상", "병", "진단", or "수술" as raw_value; copy the smallest complete meaningful phrase.
- If medication use is stated but its name is unknown, copy the complete answer as needs_confirmation and invent no drug.
- Never put an unanswered question in a confirmed field.
- review_of_systems contains patient-reported symptom assertions from the encounter, including spontaneous chief/HPI symptoms and targeted review answers. Mirror the same grounded symptom into review_of_systems while keeping it in chief_complaint/history_of_present_illness; preserve the identical source evidence. Include positive, explicitly denied, and uncertain symptoms. Exclude diagnoses, medications, tests/plans, and clinician-observed physical findings.
- Never infer physical_examination from a symptom or patient statement. Include it only when the dialogue explicitly states a clinician-observed or measured examination finding.
- For impression, preserve confirmed, suspected, and rule-out certainty. A suspected or rule-out diagnosis must keep its uncertainty wording and use needs_confirmation.
- impression contains only an explicitly stated diagnosis, suspected diagnosis, or differential. A symptom name or abnormal measurement alone is not an impression.
- Local dictionaries, not Gemma, own standard terminology and KCD codes. Do not invent or assign a standard term or KCD code.

Value V is {"raw_value":"exact supported phrase","status":"confirmed|needs_confirmation","evidence":{"source_segment_id":"seg_0001"}}. Use confirmed only for explicit facts and needs_confirmation for uncertainty. Omit any unmentioned value or empty section; the validator supplies not_mentioned defaults. For a genuinely unanswered question only, add {"source_segment_id":"seg_0001","topic":"medications"} to unresolved_questions.
