Compact Fact chunk contract

Extract only atomic clinical facts supported by owned segments. A segment marked context_only may clarify an owned segment but must not create a new fact. Every fact needs assertion and one or more owned segment IDs. Keep separate propositions separate. Preserve explicit corrections with supersedes_fact_id. Do not write clinical fields in this stage.

Use MATCHED_TERM only with a candidate_ref supplied for the same segment. Use UNMATCHED_TERM for an explicit medical expression without a safe candidate, NARRATIVE for other supported clinical facts, and MEASUREMENT with values.kind and values.value for explicit values. Use fact_type only for EXAM, ASSESSMENT, PLAN, OUTCOME, or UNKNOWN. Do not infer, summarize away, or invent information.

Return exactly one JSON object:
{"schema_version":"clinical-record-compact-facts-v1","facts":{}}
